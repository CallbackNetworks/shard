# Getting around

Shard is a graph. Everything in it — a project, a task, a decision, an identity —
is a **node**, and the lines between them are **relations**. You do not have to
think about that to use it, but it is why one screen can show you work filed under
an organization, an identity and a project at the same time.

## The Overview

The home screen collects everything asking for attention. It is not a summary you
read and leave: **every number and every line on it is a way to reach the thing it
names.**

![The Overview](/guide/01-overview.png)

- The four cards at the top are questions. Clicking *Overdue* does not open a
  report — it shows you the overdue work itself.
- The priority lanes (Critical / In Motion / Waiting / Done today) list specific
  tasks. Clicking one opens it **inside its project**, with the row scrolled to and
  briefly highlighted, so you never land on a board wondering which card you asked
  about.
- The live signals feed on the right is the activity log. Each line names something
  that happened; clicking it goes there.

![The Overview narrowed to overdue work](/guide/02-overview-overdue.png)

When a card narrows the list, the page says so and offers the way back out. The
narrowing lives in the address bar, so it survives a reload and can be handed to
someone else.

## The rail

The left rail is grouped by what you are doing: **Operate** (what is happening),
**Think** (goals, decisions, the assistant), **Build** (integrations, rules, keys),
**Data** (the graph itself), **System**.

Two things are not in the rail on purpose. **Which project** you want is a choice,
not a fixed destination, so it lives in the command palette — press `g` then `p`,
or `⌘K`. And **identity focus** is one control with many values rather than one row
per identity, so it sits in its own slot above the modules.

Press `?` at any time for the full list of keyboard shortcuts.
