# Goals

A task is something you do. A goal is something you want to be true by a date, and
it usually needs several projects' worth of tasks to get there.

![Goals](/guide/20-goals.png)

## Making one

Click *New goal*. Give it a title — "Ship the beta", "Replace the accounting
system" — and a target date. Then tick which projects count towards it.

## The progress bar updates itself

The bar on a goal card is calculated from every project linked to the goal. You
never move it by hand, and it cannot disagree with the projects it is made of.

This is the point of goals: a project's own progress tells you how that project is
going, and it cannot tell you whether the *outcome* is on track when the outcome
needs four of them.

## Attaching individual tasks

Sometimes a goal needs one specific task rather than a whole project. The box at the
bottom of a goal card adds tasks directly, and they count towards it the same way.

## The three states

Active, achieved, missed. The tabs at the top filter by state and show how many are
in each.

The number of *active* goals is worth watching. Goals are cheap to create and easy
to accumulate, and a list of fifteen active goals is a list that nobody is using.

## Where goals show up elsewhere

- The **Overview** shows your open goals in the right-hand column, so a goal with no
  movement is visible without going looking for it.
- The **daily and weekly summary emails** include them.
- The **assistant** can read them, so "am I on track for the beta?" is a question you
  can ask in plain language.
